📘 How to Play CodeMaster

        
      


      

        

          
🎯 4 Challenge Types

          
Quiz: Test coding concepts. Find Error: Spot the bug. Missing Code: Fill the blank ____. Output: Predict what the code prints.

        

        

          
⏱️ Countdown & Points

          
Easy: 100 pts (30s) • Medium: 150 pts (25s) • Hard: 200 pts (20s). Build consecutive streaks for +25 bonus points per streak level!

        

        

          
⌨️ Keyboard Shortcuts

          
Press keys 1–4 (or A–D) to select an option quickly, H for a hint, and Enter to advance to the next challenge.

        

        

          
🏆 Local Leaderboard

          
Your best scores are automatically saved in your browser's localStorage. Switch between Dark and Light modes anytime from the header.
